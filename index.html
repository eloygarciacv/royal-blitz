Royal Blitz

Move the pieces on the game board to match the positions shown on the target board. You have 2 minutes to complete as many levels as you can.

Select a piece to see its available moves. Each piece moves like in a chess game. When a piece is in the correct position, it will change its color.

Once all the pieces are in the correct positions, you can advance to the next level.

King

One square. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal.

Queen

Many squares. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal.

Rook

Many squares. Horizontal, vertical.

Bishop

Many squares. Diagonal.

Knight

L shape. Jump other pieces. Horizontal, vertical.

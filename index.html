The Digital Study

In The Digital Study, you move the pieces in your game board to match the pieces in the end board. You have a total of 2 minutes to complete as much levels as you can.

Pieces moves as in a chess game. When a piece is position, it will change its color to match the end board.

King

One square. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal.

Queen

Many squares. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal.

Rook

Many squares. Horizontal, vertical.

Bishop

Many squares. Diagonal.

Knight

L shape. Jump other pieces. Horizontal, vertical.
